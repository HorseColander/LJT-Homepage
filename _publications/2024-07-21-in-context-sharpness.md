Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, **Junteng Liu**, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, Junxian He

*ICML 2024*. [[paper]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.01548) [[code]](https://github.com/hkust-nlp/Activation_Decoding)

We discover a pattern associated with hallucinations: correct generations tend to have sharper context activations in the hidden states of the in-context tokens. We leverage this signal to mitigate hallucinations.
